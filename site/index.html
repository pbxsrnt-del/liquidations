How to buy

Same goods, three ways out the door. Pick the one that matches how much you want to carry.

By the piece

Walk in · pay the tag

Everything on the racks and shelves is individually tagged with the store ticket and our price.

  • Apparel priced at 45% of the original ticket
  • Tag photo on every listed piece
  • Small goods ship, bedding is pickup

By the bag

Mixed bundles · flat price

We sort apparel into bags as it is processed. Buy a bag and you get the mix at a lower rate per piece than the rack.

  • Sorted by size band and category
  • Contents listed before you buy
  • Good for booth and market sellers

By the pallet

Wholesale · by appointment

Unprocessed pallets go straight back out, manifest included, at a price built off the ticket retail rather than the piece count.

  • Full manifest handed over with the load
  • Load out from the dock
  • Standing lots for repeat buyers
